FAQ

Common questions, answered honestly.

How are your HSE audits different from what my team already does? +
Internal teams are close to their own operations — which is exactly why they miss things. I bring 20 years of official inspection judgment, an outside perspective, and an action plan prioritized by actual risk, not office politics. What I deliver is findings you can act on the same week.
Which industries do you serve? +
My deepest experience is in electricity, oil & gas, and petrochemical facilities, plus trade unions and small-to-mid businesses. If your industry carries real physical risk, I've likely inspected something like it.
Will my resume pass ATS filters? +
No one can guarantee a pass rate — it depends on the specific system and how closely your background matches the role. What I guarantee is a format that parses cleanly and language aligned to the keywords in the job description you give me.
How long does a resume rewrite take? +
Premium rewrites are delivered within 48 hours of payment and files arriving. Audits are typically same-day. Templates are sent within a few hours.
How do I pay, and is it secure? +
Payment is accepted via the TON network (USDT) for borderless settlement. It's fast and settles without a payment processor taking a cut. If TON isn't practical for you, email me first — I can usually arrange an alternative.
What if something isn't right? +
If I haven't started work on your order, you get a full refund, no questions asked. Once a deliverable is done, it's final — but email me if something's wrong and I'll fix it rather than walk away.
