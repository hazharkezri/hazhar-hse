4. Emergency Procedures

Alert & Evacuate: On alarm, stop work, secure hazardous processes, evacuate by nearest safe route, proceed to assembly point. Do not re-enter until given the all-clear.

Roles:

  • Emergency Coordinator: Activate the plan, direct response, contact services, account for personnel.
  • First Aid Responder: Provide initial medical care.
  • Fire/Action Team (if any): Fight small fires only with training.
  • All Personnel: Evacuate calmly, assist visitors, report missing persons.
