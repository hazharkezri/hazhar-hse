Internal Audit Checklist — ISO 45001:2018

Audit each clause. Record evidence and findings. Status: C = Conform, NC = Non-conformance, O = Observation, N/A = Not applicable.

Clause 4 — Context of the Organization

ClauseAudit QuestionStatusEvidence / Finding
4.1Have external and internal issues relevant to OH&S been identified?
4.2Are the needs and expectations of interested parties identified?
4.3Is the OH&S management system scope defined and documented?
4.4Is the OH&S management system established, implemented, and maintained?

Clause 5 — Leadership & Worker Participation

ClauseAudit QuestionStatusEvidence / Finding
5.1Has top management demonstrated leadership and commitment?
5.2Is the OH&S policy established and communicated?
5.3Are roles, responsibilities, and authorities defined and communicated?
5.4Is there a process for worker consultation and participation?

Clause 6 — Planning

ClauseAudit QuestionStatusEvidence / Finding
6.1.2Are hazards identified and risks assessed?
6.1.3Is there a process to determine and evaluate legal and other requirements?
6.1.4Are actions planned to address risks and opportunities?
6.2Are OH&S objectives set, measurable, and monitored?

Clause 7 — Support

ClauseAudit QuestionStatusEvidence / Finding
7.1Are resources (people, infrastructure) available?
7.2Are persons competent; is training effective and recorded?
7.3Is OH&S awareness raised across the organization?
7.4Are internal and external communications effective?
7.5Is documented information controlled and retained?

Clause 8 — Operation

ClauseAudit QuestionStatusEvidence / Finding
8.1Are operational controls in place for identified risks?
8.1.2Is the change management process (temporary/permanent) controlled?
8.1.3Are procurement and contractors managed for OH&S?
8.2Is emergency preparedness and response established and tested?

Clause 9 — Performance Evaluation

ClauseAudit QuestionStatusEvidence / Finding
9.1Is the process to monitor, measure, analyze, and evaluate performance defined?
9.2Are internal audits planned and performed at planned intervals?
9.3Is management review conducted and actions taken?

Clause 10 — Improvement

ClauseAudit QuestionStatusEvidence / Finding
10.2Are incidents investigated and corrective actions taken to prevent recurrence?
10.3Is there a process for continual improvement of the OH&S system?
