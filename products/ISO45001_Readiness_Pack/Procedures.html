Key OH&S Management Procedures

Standard operating procedures that support an ISO 45001-aligned system. Customize each to your operation.

1. Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment & Risk Control

Purpose: To systematically identify hazards and control risks across all activities.

Procedure:

  1. Identify tasks and associated hazards (walk-through, task analysis, incident history).
  2. Score risks using the risk matrix; determine rating.
  3. Apply the hierarchy of controls (Elimination, Substitution, Engineering, Administrative, PPE).
  4. Record in the risk register and assign responsible persons.
  5. Review at least annually or after significant change/incident.

2. Incident Reporting & Investigation

Purpose: To report, investigate, and prevent recurrence of incidents.

Procedure:

  1. Protect the scene; provide first aid; notify the HSE manager immediately.
  2. Complete the incident report form within 24 hours.
  3. Investigate root cause (5-Whys, Ishikawa), identify contributing factors.
  4. Define corrective & preventive actions with owners and deadlines.
  5. Track actions to closure and share lessons learned.

3. Permit to Work (PTW)

Purpose: To control high-risk work (hot work, confined space, working at height, excavation, electrical).

Procedure:

  1. Authorized person assesses the job and hazards.
  2. PTW form completed, specifying controls, duration, and validity.
  3. PTW authorization by competent person / area authority.
  4. Controls verified on site before work starts; permit displayed.
  5. PTW canceled on completion; returned to HSE for records.

4. Lockout / Tagout (LOTO)

Purpose: To prevent accidental energization during maintenance.

Procedure:

  1. Identify energy sources; shut down and isolate equipment.
  2. Apply locks and tags to isolation points.
  3. Attempt start-up to verify de-energization.
  4. Perform work; remove locks/tags only by the authorized person.
  5. Verify re-energization safely; update records.

5. Training & Competency

Purpose: To ensure all persons are competent for their roles.

Procedure:

  1. Identify training needs through job safety analysis and competency matrix.
  2. Deliver induction, refresher, and task-specific training.
  3. Maintain training records with dates, topics, and assessors.
  4. Review competency at least annually.

6. Contractor Management

Purpose: To ensure contractors work safely.

Procedure:

  1. Pre-qualify contractors (HSE records, insurance, training).
  2. Deliver site induction before work begins.
  3. Monitor contractor performance and controls.
  4. Evaluate performance on completion.
