Occupational Health & Safety (OH&S) Policy

[Company Name] is committed to providing a safe and healthy workplace for all employees, contractors, and visitors. We believe that no task or target justifies injury, ill health, or harm.

1. Policy Statement

We will take all reasonable steps to prevent work-related injury and ill health, and continually improve our OH&S management system. This policy is communicated to all persons working on our behalf and is available to interested parties.

2. Our Commitments

  • Provide a safe and healthy workplace and prevent work-related injury and ill health.
  • Identify hazards and control risks through systematic risk assessment.
  • Consult and participate with workers and their representatives.
  • Provide appropriate information, instruction, training, and supervision.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements and other requirements.
  • Set and review OH&S objectives and continually improve performance.

3. Management System

We implement and maintain an OH&S management system aligned with ISO 45001, following the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) cycle. Our system includes:

Policy & LeadershipOH&S Policy, Roles & Responsibilities
Hazard Identification & Risk AssessmentRisk Register & Matrix
Operational ControlProcedures (PTW, LOTO, etc.)
Emergency PreparednessEmergency Response Plan
Performance MonitoringInspection & Audit Checklists
Incident InvestigationIncident Report Procedure
Management Review & ImprovementManagement Review Records

4. Roles & Responsibilities

  • Top Management: provides leadership, resources, and review.
  • HSE Manager: implements the system and coordinates risk control.
  • Line Managers / Supervisors: enforce safe practices in their areas.
  • All Employees: comply with safe working practices and report hazards.

5. Review

This policy is reviewed annually, or when significant changes occur, by senior management.
