Fire Safety
- [ ] Fire extinguishers charged and in date
- [ ] Fire extinguishers accessible (no obstructions)
- [ ] Exit routes clear and unobstructed
- [ ] Exit doors unlocked from inside

Working at Height
- [ ] Guardrails on all elevated platforms
- [ ] Ladders in good condition
- [ ] Fall protection worn above 6 ft

Electrical
- [ ] Safe wiring, no frayed cables
- [ ] Ground fault circuit interrupters (GFCI) where required
- [ ] Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) program in place

PPE
- [ ] Required PPE identified for each task
- [ ] Hard hats used in designated areas
- [ ] Safety glasses/woggles worn where required
